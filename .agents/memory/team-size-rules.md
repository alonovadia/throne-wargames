---
name: Team size rules
description: Distinguishes signup squad size from full match scoreboard team size.
---

Signup applications represent six-player internal squads. Completed match scoreboards represent two full teams, with between one and 48 recorded players on each team.

**Why:** The six-player squad is used only for signups and tracking groups inside the larger teams; it is not the match roster limit.

**How to apply:** Keep signup validation at exactly six members. OCR parsing, match commits, corrections, archive displays, rankings, and player statistics must support two teams with up to 48 players per side.
