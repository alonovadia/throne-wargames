---
name: Team size rules
description: Distinguishes signup squad size from full match scoreboard team size.
---

Signup applications represent six-player internal squads. Completed match scoreboards represent exactly two full teams, with between one and 48 recorded players on each team. Match team colors may be any two of Blue, Red, and Yellow.

**Why:** The six-player squad is used only for signups and tracking groups inside the larger teams; it is not the match roster limit.

**How to apply:** Keep signup validation at exactly six members. OCR parsing, match commits, corrections, archive displays, rankings, and player statistics must support any two recognized team colors with up to 48 players per side.
